      Trace a shot in three taps

      1. Add a clip, or try the sample.
      2. Pause on impact and tap the ball.
      3. Scrub to the top of the flight and tap the peak.
      4. Scrub to where it lands and tap it.
      5. Press Download video. Pick Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
